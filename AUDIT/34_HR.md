## 2. What exists that touches HR at all
1. **users** - 62 columns of which 6 are employment-adjacent (role, role_level, team_id, status,
   onboarding_completed, last_login). status CHECK(Active, Benched, On Leave, Discontinued) is
   the closest thing to an employment state, but it is a single current value with no dates and
   it conflates membership state with availability (scripts/00:56).
2. **/onboarding** - a self-service profile form for a new pending_player
   (app/onboarding/page.tsx, 15,613 B; components/profile/profile-sections.tsx, 32,502 B). It
   collects DOB, address, emergency contact, device telemetry, game profile and social handles
   with no consent step and no HR workflow behind it.
3. **user_agreements** - a legal acceptance record (33_CONTRACTS.md).
4. **attendances** - training attendance, not work attendance.

## 3. Findings

### F-HR-01 - CRITICAL: no employment relationship exists
**Evidence.** no employment / contract / department / position entity; users.role is a
PERMISSION role (admin, manager, coach, analyst, player, pending_player, tryout) used
everywhere for access decisions (08_ROLES_PERMISSIONS.md).
**Impact:** the platform cannot record who is employed by the organisation, on what terms, in
which department, under which manager, from when, or until when. Every commercial and legal
capability in the target product depends on this.
**Severity:** CRITICAL | **Confidence:** HIGH

### F-HR-02 - HIGH: the organisation cannot be represented, so HR has no container
**Evidence.** no organizations table; no organization_id on any table.
**Impact:** departments, cost centres, budgets, reporting lines and entity-level contracts all
require a legal entity. This is the same CRITICAL gap as 30_TEAMS.md F-TEAM-01, seen from the
HR angle.
**Severity:** CRITICAL | **Confidence:** HIGH

### F-HR-03 - HIGH: minors are onboarded with no safeguards
**Evidence.** /onboarding collects date_of_birth, home address, emergency contact name and
number, contact_number, and precise device telemetry (device_model, ram, fps, storage,
gyroscope_enabled, hud_layout_code, sensitivity_settings)
(lib/profile-utils.ts:180-184). There is no age gate, no guardian-consent flow, no age
verification, and no COPPA / GDPR-K / India DPDPA child-terms flow. users has no
is_minor or guardian fields.
**Impact:** a large share of the esports player base is under 18. Collecting their personal data
without guardian consent is a regulatory exposure in every target market, and a reputational one.
**Severity:** HIGH | **Confidence:** HIGH
**Recommendation:** see 92_COMPLIANCE.md.

### F-HR-04 - HIGH: the onboarding form and the API disagree, so users cannot save their own data
**Evidence.** the API allowlist is 27 fields (app/api/profile/route.ts:172-187); the UI-side
getEditableFields declares five categories totalling roughly 40 fields including
emergency_contact_name, emergency_contact_number, address, date_of_birth, social_links,
preferred_language, timezone and profile_visibility (lib/profile-utils.ts:180-215).
getEditableFields is exported but NEVER CALLED, so the UI does not use it.
**Impact:** a user fills in fields that the API silently rejects with NO_VALID_UPDATES. The
onboarding funnel appears broken and support cannot diagnose it.
**Severity:** HIGH | **Confidence:** HIGH

### F-HR-05 - MEDIUM: role is used as both a permission and a job description
**Evidence.** users.role feeds 5 permission models (08_ROLES_PERMISSIONS.md) and is also the only
statement of what a person does. players.status conflates Benched / On Leave / Discontinued.
**Impact:** a Head of Esports, a Content Producer, a Partnership Manager or a Finance Manager
cannot be represented. Granting someone admin permissions would also make them an admin as a
job.
**Severity:** MEDIUM | **Confidence:** HIGH

### F-HR-06 - MEDIUM: no absence or leave concept
**Evidence.** attendances tracks training attendance with status present / late / absent / auto
(scripts/00:248). There is no leave request, no entitlement, no approval, no sick-note concept.
**Impact:** a player on medical leave is indistinguishable from a player who skipped practice.
This matters both operationally and legally.
**Severity:** MEDIUM | **Confidence:** HIGH

### F-HR-07 - MEDIUM: no performance review or goal concept
**Evidence.** absence. The "AI insights" in /dashboard/analytics are deterministic performance
STATISTICS rules about gameplay (components/analytics/analytics-sections.tsx), not reviews of a
person.
**Impact:** no probation review, no development plan, no performance management - a core HR
function and a common reason an organisation buys an OS.
**Severity:** MEDIUM | **Confidence:** HIGH

## 4. Verdict
**BUILD NEW as a first-class domain.** V2 requirements are specified in 64_FUTURE_HR.md. Key
decisions: (a) separate the HRIS from the competitive-operations core; (b) model employment as an
effective-dated Engagement between a Person and an Organization (see 62_PEOPLE_MODEL.md);
(c) make minors a first-class case with guardian linkage and consent records; (d) keep payroll
execution outside the platform (65_FUTURE_PAYROLL.md); (e) integrate with an external HRIS for
anything statutory.
