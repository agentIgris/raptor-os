# 73 - FUTURE DOCUMENTS (requirements only - NOT implemented)
**Audit.md section:** 80 (SECTION 77)
Covers documents, versions, permissions, expiration, signatures, approvals and audit.

## 1. Starting position
There is no document store. Agreement text is a JSON string in a **world-writable** key/value
table, and acceptance records a version integer with no content hash (`33_CONTRACTS.md`).

## 2. The invariant that fixes the legacy defect
**An acceptance must reference an immutable, content-hashed document version.** The legacy system
records only an integer, so if the content is edited without bumping the version, the
organisation cannot prove what was accepted (`33_CONTRACTS.md` F-CON-02). This is the single most
important requirement in this document.

## 3. Requirements
### 3.1 Document and version
* `Document`: `org_id`, `type` (contract | policy | nda | agreement_template | board_minutes |
  invoice | travel_doc | identity | report | deliverable_proof), `title`, `owner`, `status`
  (draft | in_review | approved | published | superseded | archived), `current_version_id`,
  `effective_from`, `effective_to`, `expiry_at`, `retention_class`.
* `DocumentVersion`: `document_id`, `version_number`, `content_asset_id` (the file), or a
  `template_body` for generated text, **`content_sha256`**, `authored_by`, `authored_at`,
  `approved_by`, `approved_at`, `change_note`, `jurisdiction`, `language`. **Immutable once
  approved** - a change creates a new version.
* Generated documents (contracts, reports, itineraries) are produced from a versioned template
  and stored as a version, so the exact artefact sent is retrievable forever.

### 3.2 Contract lifecycle
`Contract` builds on `Document`:
* `contract` + `version` + `parties` (counterparty organisation and signatory `Person`s, or the
  organisation's authorised signatory) + `engagement_id` + `effective_from` / `effective_to` +
  `governing_law` + `termination_terms` + `status` (draft | in_review | awaiting_signature |
  active | expiring | terminated | expired).
* `ContractCompensationTerm` links to `CompensationAgreement` (`65_FUTURE_PAYROLL.md`).
* `ContractObligation` links to `InventoryItem` for commercial deals (`70_FUTURE_SPONSORSHIP.md`).
* **Expiry detection** drives the `ContractExpiring` event (`77_EVENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`) and a
  reminder workflow.

### 3.3 Templates
* `DocumentTemplate` per document type and jurisdiction, with variables and a merge schema.
* **Machine translation is prohibited for legal documents.** Each locale requires a separately
  drafted and separately approved version (`59_INTERNATIONALIZATION.md`).
* Templates are versioned, so a contract generated last year can be regenerated byte-identically.

### 3.4 Permissions and access
* Access is granted by an explicit `AccessGrant`: `document_id` or `version_id`, `grantee_type`
  (person | role | engagement | external party with an expiry), `grantee_id`, `permission`
  (view | comment | sign | manage), `valid_from`, `valid_to`.
* **External sharing** is the norm in this domain: a player, a sponsor or a minor's guardian needs
  to see one document and nothing else. This requires per-document grants, not role-based access
  to a bucket.
* Every access is audit-logged, including reads of documents containing personal data
  (`49_AUDIT_LOGGING.md` §3).
* Identity documents and minors' documents are stored **encrypted**, with a separate, stricter
  access policy and an access log.

### 3.5 Signatures
* `SignatureRequest`: `document_version_id`, `signer_type` (internal | external), `signer` (a
  `Person` or an external contact with a verified email), `order` (sequential or parallel),
  `expires_at`, `status`, `provider`, `provider_envelope_id`, `completed_at`.
* **Two options:** (a) integrate an e-signature provider (DocuSign, Dropbox Sign, Adobe Sign) -
  recommended for commercial contracts; (b) an internal flow with an OTP/identity check and a
  verifiable artefact (document hash + signer identity + timestamp + IP + a signed completion
  record) - acceptable for low-value internal policies.
* **Either way the platform stores the artefact**, not just the status, so the signature remains
  verifiable after the provider relationship ends.
* `Acceptance`: for versioned policy/agreement acceptance, recording
  `person_id`, `document_version_id`, `content_sha256`, `accepted_at`, `ip`, `user_agent`,
  `method`. **Re-consent is triggered by a new version** (`77_EVENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`).

### 3.6 Approvals
* `Approval` per document version and per business object: `approver_engagement_id`, `decision`,
  `reason`, `decided_at`, `stage`. Delegation and thresholds from the policy layer
  (`63_ROLE_MODEL.md`).
* Segregation of duties enforced by policy (`requester != approver`).

### 3.7 Expiration and retention
* `expiry_at` on contracts, policies, visas, insurance, licences and agreements, with configurable
  reminder schedules and an escalation path.
* Retention per document class (`50_DATA_RETENTION.md` §3), with `LegalHold` blocking any purge.
* Expiry does **not** delete; it transitions status and notifies.

## 4. Security requirements
* Private storage; short-lived signed URLs; no public document URLs.
* Content sniffing and malware scanning on upload; PDF sanitisation.
* Documents rendered for viewing must be served with a restrictive `Content-Type` and
  `Content-Disposition`, from a separate origin or with `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox`, so a
  malicious document cannot execute in the app's origin.
* Every view, download, share and signature is audit-logged with the actor and the IP.
* Data-subject access: a person can see every document concerning them.

## 5. Migration from the legacy system
* `admin_config.agreement_content_*` -> `Document` + `DocumentVersion`, **with the caveat that the
  historic content is not trustworthy** (the table was world-writable -
  `13_RLS_SECURITY.md` F-RLS-04). Historic acceptances are archived as evidence with a clear
  "provenance unverified" flag; **a re-consent campaign is required.**
* `user_agreements` -> `Acceptance` rows, but they cannot be given a content hash, so they cannot
  be treated as legally sufficient going forward.
* `public/` files and the `media` bucket -> `Asset(kind=document|media)`.

## 6. Priority
**Very high.** Contracts, agreements and expiry are prerequisites for HR, payroll, partnerships and
compliance. This is the module that most directly determines whether an organisation can trust the
product with its legal paperwork.
